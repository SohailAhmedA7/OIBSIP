01

About Him

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam was an Indian aerospace scientist and the 11th President of India. He played an important role in India's space and missile development programmes.

Born on 15 October 1931 in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu, he came from a humble background and went on to become one of India's most respected scientists and public figures.

Beyond his scientific contributions, Dr. Kalam was known for his dedication to education, young people and the development of India. He inspired millions of students to dream big and work towards meaningful goals.
